# Explore a decision

Use the Rewired workspace routes to read relevant context. Identify the question the user needs answered and what would make the conversation useful: a recommendation, a comparison, a decision brief, or a short list of unknowns.

Present what can already be concluded. Ask one question only when its answer changes the decision or resolves a dependency. Offer examples where helpful. Do not require a lengthy interview before providing useful analysis.

Compare realistic alternatives against the user's stated goals and constraints. Distinguish evidence, assumptions, tradeoffs, and your recommendation. A user may pause or stop at any point. Stop when the requested outcome is reached or progress depends on evidence that is unavailable; explain the smallest next check.

For an explicitly requested saved exploration, checkpoint confirmed answers in a unique dated file under `work/discovery/` and link it from `work/index.md`. If saving fails, retain an unsaved recap rather than claiming a checkpoint. Update the user's profile only when context-building is requested and the facts are confirmed.

A recommendation remains a recommendation until accepted. Record an accepted decision when requested, including its rationale and unresolved conditions. Do not send messages, change accounts, schedule work, or execute a proposal merely because it emerged in the discussion.
