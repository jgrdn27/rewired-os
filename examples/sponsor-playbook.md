# Sponsor campaign: fictional practice method

This example contains no live campaign or publishing authorization. Use it only when the user selects a demo or asks to adapt it to their own process.

## Useful result

Deliver one sponsored piece through preparation, review, publication, and a dated results report. A request for a draft can stop after drafting with later checkpoints explicitly unfinished.

## What the work needs

A campaign brief, permitted product claims and supporting sources, the selected audience and channel, and any known review requirements. Confirm missing commercial commitments rather than inventing them. Use the existing content calendar or campaign tool when it owns progress.

## Checkpoints

| Checkpoint | Required input or prior result | Deliverable | Completion check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brief | User's requested outcome and selected source material | Campaign brief | Audience, channel, deliverable, claim boundaries, and unresolved commitments are explicit. |
| Draft | Brief and supporting product evidence | Versioned copy or asset | Draft satisfies the brief and its factual claims have support. |
| Review | Exact draft version and known review requirements | Dated review outcome | Required reviewer decisions identify the version they cover; any remaining changes are explicit. |
| Publication | Reviewed version, destination, and authorization to publish | Publication record | Actual publication is verified at the intended destination; an upload or submission alone is insufficient. |
| Results | Verified publication and an agreed measurement window | Dated results note | Report identifies source, period, metrics available, and limitations. Missing data stays missing. |

## Improve the next occasion

Compare the requested outcome with observed results and the effort needed to deliver. Retain one supported learning or a concrete unresolved question. Change the reusable method only when requested or when an accepted correction requires it.
