# A library people can trust

The stock recommendations are lemlist, FullEnrich, Clay, and Claap, selected by Jérémy. Claap is the default meeting recorder. User-selected alternatives remain first-class choices and do not need public catalog admission for private use. Inclusion means a reviewed use case and documented route, not a universal best-tool claim. The preview has documentation-reviewed entries; live pilots remain outstanding.

## Admission and maintenance

Every listing needs an identifiable operator, official sources, a concrete useful workflow, prerequisites, data destinations, access boundaries, a read-only verification step, cost mechanics, failure/recovery guidance, licensing treatment, and an owner/check date. Record tested host and tool versions. A source review and a successful account pilot are separate evidence levels.

Proposed maintenance policy: review listings every 30 days and after a reported failure or vendor interface change. No maintenance job ships scheduled. Before installing or doing consequential work, recheck current official instructions regardless of the interval. Mark stale entries as needing review; disable an unsafe/broken setup route without deleting the user's saved knowledge.

Prefer user-owned tools that already satisfy the task. Compare alternatives by fit, output quality, account access, total cost, portability, and reliability. Do not force additional subscriptions or reward longer tool chains. Record when a free/manual method is enough.

## Future commercial listings

Paid listings are a possible future business model, not an existing relationship. A listing must pass the same technical and editorial criteria regardless of payment. Clearly label sponsorship and affiliate links next to the recommendation. Record the commercial relationship separately from verification status. Payment must not buy undisclosed ranking, fabricated benchmarks, claims of account access, or exclusive routing.

Keep partner promotional material separate from the user's operating guidance. Sponsors receive no user files, prospect data, telemetry, or contact details merely by appearing in the library. A vendor can provide an update for review; it cannot remotely replace installed instructions without the user's update policy.

The catalog uses `commercial_relationship: "not_configured"` in this stock preview. That describes package configuration, not a claim about all of the creator's business relationships.
