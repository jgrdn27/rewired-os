# Prepare an experiment and learn from it

Use when a founder or operator needs a grounded outbound draft or a review of completed outreach. Original Rewired method; no promised response-rate benchmark.

1. Read the target brief, offer, selected evidence, and relevant voice preferences. Pick one audience/problem hypothesis and a useful response goal. Do not turn a weak inference into a personalized factual claim.
2. Prepare a compact message and follow-up rationale using claims that can be supported. Keep the user's review preference. Show a representative draft and flag uncertain claims.
3. If campaign preparation is requested, read the selected tool profile and live schema. Map required fields, check missing values, deduplicate, and preserve suppression/opt-out rules in the destination. Separate local drafting, saving a platform draft, adding contacts, and sending; authorization for one does not imply the others.
4. Before an authorized launch, verify the actual audience, sender, sequence, timing, limits, and current platform requirements. If launch requires the app, leave the prepared artifact and exact handoff. Do not claim the assistant launched it.
5. When results exist, compare comparable cohorts and meaningful replies/opportunities, report denominators and observation windows, and identify data-quality or selection biases. Do not invent causal certainty from a small test.
6. Save one supported learning, one unresolved hypothesis, and the next proposed change. Link results from the wiki after normal ingestion rather than copying a whole campaign export into every chat.

Deliverable: a reviewable experiment brief or an evidence-backed result review. Drafting, scheduling, and sending are reported as distinct states.
