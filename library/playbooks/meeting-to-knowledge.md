# Turn a meeting into useful knowledge

Use for sales discovery, customer feedback, onboarding, or other selected GTM meetings. The method stays the same when the recording tool changes. Claap is the stock recommendation; follow [tool choice](../tool-choice.md) for an existing or preferred alternative.

1. Establish the requested outcome and meeting scope: one call, a defined account, or a bounded period. Reuse the intended business/workspace from context. Distinguish configuring future recording from reading existing meetings; one is not permission for the other.
2. Retrieve metadata first to locate the correct source, then the transcript or necessary excerpts. Record the provider, workspace, recording ID/URL, meeting date, speakers where supplied, capture time, and coverage. A search snippet or generated recap cannot establish that the full call was reviewed.
3. Answer a question from selected excerpts when that is sufficient. For requested wiki ingestion, export and read the complete selected source through the normal knowledge workflow; preserve original bytes where available. A partial export remains partial and must not be registered as a complete transcript. Cloud-only sessions contribute a labeled handoff for the designated writer.
4. Separate customer statements, commitments, objections, decisions, hypotheses, and assistant suggestions. Quote only supported wording and keep timestamp/source references when available. Attribute action items to a speaker or confirmed owner; do not invent ownership or deadlines from a summary.
5. Update relevant knowledge about the account, buyer problem, messaging, or product feedback. Route a deliverable and next unfinished step into work when appropriate. Keep live deal state in the authoritative system; mention a proposed CRM update rather than silently applying it.
6. Preserve each source version. Match duplicates using provider/workspace/recording identity and content/revision evidence. Treat later corrected transcripts as revisions, not another independent corroborating meeting. Do not upload media when a transcript is sufficient.

Deliverable: a sourced meeting brief or wiki update, explicit unknowns, and a useful next step. Follow-up messages remain drafts until sending is requested. Recurring imports require a supported schedule and explicit scope; inclusion of this playbook does not start one.
