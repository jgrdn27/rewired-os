# Choose a target worth pursuing

Use when an operator needs a defensible account segment, not just a larger list. Original Rewired method; validate it against the user's actual results.

1. Read the relevant business context and current objective. Establish the offer, buyer problem, reachable market, and useful outcome. Ask only about missing decisions that affect targeting.
2. Review selected customer evidence: calls, wins/losses, product usage, or research. Separate observed fit from hypotheses. A role title or funding event alone is not proof of need.
3. Produce one segment hypothesis with inclusion criteria, exclusions, buying roles, observable signals, and evidence links. Identify the one assumption most likely to invalidate it.
4. Define a small representative review sample and the fields needed to assess it. Reuse an existing list before adding a tool. If scaling research is justified, consult the relevant tool profile; do not purchase data just to complete this method.
5. Review examples against the criteria, record false positives and missing evidence, and adjust the hypothesis. Save a target brief and next test in the work index.

Deliverable: a short segment brief, evidence table, explicit unknowns, and a testable next step. Do not label assistant guesses as the user's approved ICP or invent market size, intent, or conversion forecasts.
