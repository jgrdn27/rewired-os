## Useful for

Up to three concrete questions this source helps answer, when useful for retrieval. Do not infer claims merely to improve search matching.

## Evidence and limits

Summarize supported statements with source locations or timestamps where available. Distinguish reported results, confirmed decisions, proposals, and open questions. Keep material uncertainty, scope, and relevant dates.

## Freshness and revisions

Identify what the source can establish as of its event date. Link an explicit revision or conflicting source; explain which claim changes and what remains unresolved. For a claim checked against a current authoritative source, record the evidence and actual check date. Otherwise write unverified; reading or ingesting a source is not independent verification.
